Privacy policy

Version 1.0 · Effective November 3, 2026

1. Who is responsible

José Díaz, from Panama, is responsible for your data in Stocky.

For any privacy matter, write to us at ….

2. What data we process

  • Your account: first name, last name, email, phone, your business name (if you add it), country and language.
  • Your password: it is stored encrypted; no one can read it.
  • If you log in with Google: we only use your Google name and email. We don’t use your profile photo or your contacts.
  • What you enter: orders, items, photos, customers, payments, sales, expenses, deposits and catalogs.
  • Acceptance record: which text you accepted, its version, the date and time, how you accepted it and whether you use Android or iPhone.
  • Your preferences (theme, currency, lock): they stay on your phone.

3. What we use it for

  • To create and manage your account, and let you log in.
  • To store your data, back it up in the cloud and have it on all your phones.
  • To create your receipts, catalogs and the copy of your data.
  • To recover your password and reply when you write to us.
  • To prove what you accepted, as the law requires.

We do this with your consent (the boxes when you create your account) and to provide the service in the Terms. We don’t sell your data and we don’t use it for advertising.

4. Where it is stored

  • On your phone: everything you use in the app. If you use Stocky as a guest, your data stays only on your phone.
  • In the cloud: with an account, your data, your photos and the acceptance record are copied to Supabase. Supabase is our provider: it stores the data on our behalf (data processor).
  • Supabase’s servers are in the United States (us-east-1 region). That is an international data transfer. By accepting this policy, you give your consent to that transfer.

5. Ads

The free version shows ads. Today they are samples made by the app itself: they don’t use your data or send it to anyone.

When we turn on real ads (for example, from Google AdMob), we will first update this policy with the data they use, and ask you to accept it again.

6. Statistics, alerts and cookies

  • In the “Your privacy” notice and in Settings › Privacy you can turn “Usage statistics” and “Alerts and news” on or off. They start off.
  • Today Stocky doesn’t collect usage statistics or send notifications: we only keep what you chose, and when. If we ever use them, it will only be with your permission.
  • The app doesn’t use cookies. Our website doesn’t use cookies or trackers either; it is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may record technical data about visits (such as the IP address) for security.

7. Phone permissions

  • Camera and photos: only when you tap “Take photo” or “Choose from gallery”.
  • Fingerprint or face (auto-lock): your phone checks it. Stocky never receives your fingerprint or your face.
  • Share: it only opens when you decide to send a receipt, a catalog or your data.

8. How long we keep it

  • As long as you have your account.
  • If you delete a photo in the app, its copy in the cloud is kept until you delete your account.
  • When you delete your account from the app, your account, your data, your photos and the acceptance record are erased from the cloud right away, along with that account’s data on that phone.
  • On other phones where you logged in, the session is closed, but what is saved there isn’t erased from the app: it is erased when you uninstall it from that phone.
  • If you ask us by email, at …, to delete it, we do it within 30 days at most.

9. Your rights

You can access your data, correct it, delete it, object to a use and take a copy (portability). You can also withdraw your consent whenever you want.

  • Correct: Settings › Personal details.
  • Copy: Settings › Your data › Download my data.
  • Delete: Settings › Your data › Delete my account.
  • Permissions: Settings › Privacy.
  • Or write to us at ….

If you are not satisfied, you can go to ANTAI (Panama’s National Authority for Transparency and Access to Information).

10. Security

  • The connection to the cloud is encrypted.
  • Your password is stored encrypted.
  • Each account can only see its own data and photos.
  • You can turn on auto-lock in Settings.
  • If something happens that puts your data at risk, we will notify you and the authority, as the law requires.

11. Minors

Stocky is not made for people under 18, and they should not use it.

12. Applicable law and changes

This policy follows Panama’s Law 81 of 2019 on Personal Data Protection and its regulations, Executive Decree 285 of 2021.

If we change it in an important way, we will let you know in the app and ask you to accept it again.
