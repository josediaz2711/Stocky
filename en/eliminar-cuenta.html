Delete your account

You can delete your Stocky account and its data whenever you want. Here’s how:

From the app

  1. Open Stocky and log in with your account.
  2. Tap “Settings” in the bottom bar.
  3. Under “Your data”, tap “Delete my account”.
  4. If you want to keep a copy of your data, first tap “Download my data”.
  5. In “Enter your password to confirm”, type your password. If you log in with Google, type your email in “Type your email to confirm”.
  6. Slide “Slide to delete your account”.

It is deleted right away (you need internet). When it’s done you will see “Your account was deleted.”

If you no longer have the app

Write to us at … from your account’s email, with the subject “Delete my account”. If you write from another email, we will ask you to confirm that the account is yours. We delete it within 30 days at most and reply when it’s done.

What is deleted

  • Your account: first name, last name, email, phone, business name, country and language. You will no longer be able to log in with it.
  • Your data in the cloud: orders, items, customers, payments, sales, expenses and transactions.
  • Your photos in the cloud: those of your items, your catalogs and your profile photo.
  • The record of what you accepted (Terms and Privacy).
  • If you do it from the app, also that account’s data on that phone.

What is not deleted

  • What is saved on other phones where you logged in with the account: the session is closed there, and it is deleted when you uninstall Stocky from that phone.
  • Files you already downloaded or shared (receipts, catalogs, the copy of your data): they are on your phone or with whoever received them.
  • If you used Stocky as a guest, without an account, your data is only on your phone and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

More details in the Privacy policy.
