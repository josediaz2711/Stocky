From the store, whenever you want (Google Play › Subscriptions, or iPhone Settings › Subscriptions). You keep Pro until the end of the paid period. If there is a free trial, charging starts when the trial ends unless you cancel before.

4. Your rights

This policy doesn’t take away your rights under Law 45 of 2007 on consumer protection. You can file a complaint with ACODECO.
